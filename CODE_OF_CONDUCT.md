# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

InsightOS aims to be a respectful, constructive space for collaboration. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to participate in ways that make the project welcoming and productive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Focus criticism on work, evidence, and decisions rather than individuals.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository owner through a verified contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue and pull request discussions, and other spaces where a person is representing InsightOS.
